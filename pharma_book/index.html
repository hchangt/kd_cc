About This Book

Over four decades, I have watched brilliant scientists fail to bring life-saving drugs to patients — not because the science was wrong, but because no one taught them how the industry actually works. This book is my attempt to fix that.

Written with colleagues from Eli Lilly, Johnson & Johnson, GSK, AbbVie, Novo Nordisk, Roche, Pfizer, AstraZeneca, Novartis, Merck, BMS, and Gilead — and with faculty from Harvard, MIT, and Stanford — this is the guide we wished existed when we started.

Every chapter covers one domain in depth: the real science, the actual process, the insider tricks, and the hard lessons learned from programs that succeeded and ones that failed. Read it cover-to-cover, or jump to the function you need right now.

The final four chapters trace five landmark drugs — Keytruda, Ozempic, Mounjaro, Biktarvy, Humira, and Herceptin — end-to-end from molecule to multibillion-dollar market.

How to Use This Book

New to Pharma

Read Chapters 1–3 first, then follow the sequence. Don't skip Ch. 4 (Translational) — it's the bridge from bench to bedside.

Scientists Moving to Industry

Start with Ch. 1, then jump to Ch. 8 (Regulatory) and Ch. 10 (BD). Return to the clinical sequence once you have the business context.

Commercial Leaders

Chapters 12–14 (Market Access, Medical Affairs, Commercial) are your core. Ch. 4 (Clinical Strategy) and Ch. 22–25 (Case Studies) give you the pipeline context.

Starting a Company

Read Ch. 1, Ch. 10 (BD), Ch. 17 (Finance), Ch. 18 (AI), and Ch. 21 (Startup) first. Use the rest as a reference as you grow.
